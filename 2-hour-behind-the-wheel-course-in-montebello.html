2 Hours Behind The Wheel Course

2-Hour Behind-the-Wheel Driving Lesson

A focused driving session designed to build confidence and master essential skills for safe driving.

Vehicle Control & Safety

Understand how to operate the car safely, including:

  • Proper hand positioning on the steering wheel.
  • Smooth acceleration and braking techniques.
  • How to use mirrors effectively.
  • Pre-drive safety checks (tires, brakes, lights).

Turning, Lane Changes & Parking

Master essential maneuvers for real-world driving:

  • Safe left and right turns.
  • Smooth lane changes & blind-spot awareness.
  • Parallel parking & angle parking techniques.
  • Three-point turns and U-turns.

Traffic Laws & Road Signs

Develop a strong understanding of traffic rules:

  • Reading and understanding road signs.
  • Right-of-way rules at intersections.
  • How to handle stop signs and traffic lights.
  • Speed limits and safe driving distances.

Defensive Driving & Emergency Situations

Prepare for unexpected situations on the road:

  • Avoiding common driving mistakes.
  • Reacting to sudden stops or obstacles.
  • Driving safely in rain, fog, or low visibility.
  • Understanding pedestrian and cyclist safety.
